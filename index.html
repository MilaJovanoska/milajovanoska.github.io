About Me

Personal Bio

I'm Mila Jovanoska, currently pursuing an MSc in Robotics at the University of Manchester. I hold a BSc in Software Engineering and Information Systems from the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering (FINKI) in Skopje, graduating with a GPA of 9.82/10.00.

My background includes machine learning research, statistical analysis, full-stack software development, and teaching. I have co-authored a scientific paper published in the proceedings of CIIT 2026 and gained practical experience through academic projects, an engineering internship, and undergraduate laboratory instruction.

Research Interests & Career Objectives

My academic interests focus on robot perception, autonomous navigation, intelligent control systems, and the application of artificial intelligence and machine learning in robotics. I am particularly interested in developing reliable, autonomous systems capable of operating in complex, real-world environments.

My long-term goal is to contribute to robotics research and development, combining software engineering and intelligent technologies to address challenges in healthcare, environmental sustainability, and disaster response.
